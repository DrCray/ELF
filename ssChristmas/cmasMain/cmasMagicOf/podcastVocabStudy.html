Vocabulary Study Guide

Click on each word to see its definition and example

jump the gun
Definition: (idiom) To do something too early, before the right or expected time.
Example: Don't jump the gun. Wait until everyone arrives before you start eating.
get into the holiday spirit
Definition: (expression) To start feeling happy and excited about a holiday and the things people do to celebrate it.
Example: Baking cookies with my kids always helps me get into the holiday spirit.
go overboard
Definition: (idiom) To do something too much, or with more energy or money than is needed.
Example: We agreed not to go overboard with gifts this year.
through the roof
Definition: (idiom) Rising to a very high level, often much higher than expected.
Example: After the storm, vegetable prices went through the roof.
drive around
Definition: (phrasal verb) To drive from place to place without hurrying, often just to look at things.
Example: On Sunday afternoons, we like to drive around the countryside with no plan.
bumper-to-bumper
Definition: (collocation) Describing traffic that is so heavy the cars are very close together and moving very slowly.
Example: The highway was bumper-to-bumper all the way to the beach.
stuck in my head
Definition: (expression) Used when a song or tune keeps playing in your mind and you can't stop thinking about it.
Example: That advertising jingle has been stuck in my head all morning.
drive me up the wall
Definition: (idiom) To make someone feel very annoyed or irritated.
Example: My neighbor's barking dog is starting to drive me up the wall.
on repeat
Definition: (expression) Played again and again, many times in a row.
Example: She played her new favorite song on repeat for the whole bus ride.
settle the debate
Definition: (collocation) To finally decide an argument or question that people disagree about.
Example: Let's check the dictionary and settle the debate about how to spell it.
hands down
Definition: (idiom) Easily and without any doubt; clearly the best or the winner.
Example: This is hands down the best pizza in town.
takes me back
Definition: (phrasal verb) Makes me remember a time or place in the past.
Example: The smell of sunscreen always takes me back to summer vacations as a kid.
wait in line
Definition: (collocation) To stand in a row of people until it is your turn.
Example: We had to wait in line for two hours to buy concert tickets.
take down
Definition: (phrasal verb) To remove something that was hung up or set up, such as decorations or a tent.
Example: It took us all afternoon to take down the tent after the camping trip.
wrap up
Definition: (phrasal verb) To finish or bring something, such as a meeting or a show, to an end.
Example: Let's wrap up the meeting so everyone can go to lunch.
