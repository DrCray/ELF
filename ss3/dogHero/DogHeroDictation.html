Trusty, Dog Hero

  1. Trusty is a guide-dog that belongs to a blind-man named Harry.
  2. Trusty assists Harry to get to and from his workplace.
  3. One day there was a big explosion at Harry’s workplace.
  4. People began screaming and running in confusion.
  5. Thankfully, guide dogs for the blind are trained to remain calm in emergency situations.
  6. Harold gave Trusty a command to lead him to safety.
  7. Trusty led Harold and his co-workers down the stairwell and out of the building.
  8. Trusty was given a medal of honor for her bravery from the Fire Department.
  9. Also, the mayor gave Trusty a key to the city.
