Vocabulary Study Guide

Click on each word to see its definition and example

caught me off guard
Definition: (idiom) Surprised me; happened when I wasn't expecting it.
Example: Her resignation letter caught me off guard -- I had no idea she was unhappy.
a badge of honor
Definition: (idiom) Something to be proud of, even if it seems unusual to others.
Example: He wears his old work boots like a badge of honor, proof of years of hard work.
stood out
Definition: (phrasal verb) Was especially noticeable compared to everything else.
Example: Of all the applicants, her portfolio really stood out to the hiring team.
choke on
Definition: (phrasal verb) To have trouble swallowing or breathing because of something in your throat; also used figuratively for struggling with something difficult.
Example: He nearly choked on his coffee when he heard the surprising news.
hands-on
Definition: (expression) Involving direct, active participation rather than just watching or reading.
Example: The museum offers a hands-on exhibit where kids can build their own volcano.
makes total sense
Definition: (expression) Is completely logical or easy to understand.
Example: Once she explained her reasoning, the decision made total sense.
over the top
Definition: (idiom) Excessive or more extreme than necessary.
Example: The birthday party had fireworks and a live band -- it felt a bit over the top.
spoke to me
Definition: (expression) Resonated with me personally; felt especially meaningful.
Example: That painting really spoke to me, so I bought a print of it.
travel bug
Definition: (idiom) A strong, persistent desire to travel.
Example: Ever since her trip to Peru, she's had a serious travel bug.
blast
Definition: (expression, informal) A lot of fun.
Example: The road trip was a total blast, even when we got lost twice.
trip over
Definition: (phrasal verb) To stumble or lose one's balance on something.
Example: Be careful not to trip over the cables on the floor.
sums it up
Definition: (expression) Captures or summarizes the main point concisely.
Example: "Expect the unexpected" really sums up how I feel about traveling.
fresh start
Definition: (expression) A new beginning, often after leaving problems behind.
Example: Moving to a new city gave them both a much-needed fresh start.
giving those a miss
Definition: (expression) Choosing to skip or avoid something.
Example: I usually love dessert, but tonight I'm giving it a miss.
tuning in
Definition: (phrasal verb) Listening to or watching a broadcast, such as a podcast or radio show.
Example: Thanks for tuning in to this week's episode -- see you next time!
