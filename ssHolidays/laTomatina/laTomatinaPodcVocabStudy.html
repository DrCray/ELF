Vocabulary Study Guide

Click on each word to see its definition and example

chaos
Definition: Complete disorder and confusion.
Example: The kitchen was total chaos ten minutes before the guests arrived.
wrap my head around
Definition: (idiom) To fully understand something confusing or complicated.
Example: I still can't wrap my head around how the magician did that trick.
letting loose
Definition: (expression) Relaxing and enjoying oneself freely, without holding back.
Example: After finals ended, the students spent the whole weekend letting loose.
caught up
Definition: (expression) Fully absorbed or swept along in something.
Example: He got so caught up in the game that he missed his bus stop.
in the thick of it
Definition: (idiom) Right in the middle of a busy, intense, or chaotic situation.
Example: During the storm, the rescue crews were in the thick of it all night.
throw one back
Definition: (expression) To respond to an action by doing the same thing back, often playfully.
Example: She splashed him with water, so naturally he threw one back.
stuck with me
Definition: (expression) Stayed in my memory; left a lasting impression.
Example: One line from that speech really stuck with me for years afterward.
break down
Definition: (phrasal verb) To remove or get rid of something, such as a barrier or wall.
Example: Learning the language helped break down the barriers between the two groups.
up for it
Definition: (expression) Willing or eager to do something.
Example: We're planning a hike this weekend, are you up for it?
once in a lifetime
Definition: (idiom) Describing an experience that is extremely rare or special.
Example: Seeing a total solar eclipse felt like a once in a lifetime opportunity.
dive in
Definition: (phrasal verb) To begin something enthusiastically, without hesitating.
Example: The recipe looked complicated, but she decided to just dive in.
no joke
Definition: (idiom) Something serious, difficult, or not to be underestimated.
Example: Studying for the bar exam is no joke — it takes months of preparation.
small price to pay
Definition: (idiom) A minor cost or inconvenience that is worth accepting for a greater benefit.
Example: A little bit of jet lag is a small price to pay for seeing the world.
one for the books
Definition: (idiom) An event or experience that is especially memorable or remarkable.
Example: That championship game was truly one for the books.
bucket list
Definition: (idiom) A list of things a person hopes to do or achieve during their lifetime.
Example: Visiting Japan has been on my bucket list since I was a teenager.
