Vocabulary Study Guide

Click on each word to see its definition and example

catch up
Definition: (phrasal verb) To talk with someone you haven't seen for a while and learn what has happened in their life.
Example: Let's meet for coffee next week and catch up.
on the road
Definition: (expression) Traveling, especially by car, for a long time or a long distance.
Example: The band spent three months on the road last summer.
bumper-to-bumper
Definition: (collocation) Describes traffic that is so heavy the cars are very close together and moving very slowly.
Example: We were stuck in bumper-to-bumper traffic on the way to the airport.
the more, the merrier
Definition: (idiom) Used to say that an event will be more fun if more people join.
Example: Of course your cousin can come to the picnic. The more, the merrier!
go all out
Definition: (idiom) To make a very big effort or spend a lot of money to do something well.
Example: Our neighbors always go all out for Halloween and decorate the whole house.
dig in
Definition: (phrasal verb, informal) To start eating, often with enthusiasm.
Example: Dinner is on the table, so everybody dig in!
save room for
Definition: (expression) To eat less of one food so that you can still eat something later, usually dessert.
Example: Don't eat too much pizza. You need to save room for cake.
your eyes are bigger than your stomach
Definition: (idiom) Used when someone takes more food than they can actually eat.
Example: You ordered three desserts? I think your eyes are bigger than your stomach.
food coma
Definition: (expression, informal) A very sleepy, lazy feeling after eating a large meal.
Example: After the barbecue, the whole family was in a food coma on the grass.
polish off
Definition: (phrasal verb, informal) To finish eating or drinking all of something, often quickly.
Example: The kids managed to polish off a large pizza in ten minutes.
bargain hunters
Definition: (collocation) People who search carefully for things to buy at the lowest possible price.
Example: The flea market is popular with bargain hunters every Sunday morning.
my cup of tea
Definition: (idiom) Something you enjoy or are interested in; usually used in the negative, as in "not my cup of tea."
Example: Horror movies aren't really my cup of tea.
sleep in
Definition: (phrasal verb) To sleep later than usual in the morning, on purpose.
Example: I love to sleep in on Saturday mornings.
kick off
Definition: (phrasal verb) To start an event, activity, or period of time.
Example: The city will kick off the festival with a big parade.
holiday spirit
Definition: (collocation) A happy, friendly, generous feeling that people have during a holiday season.
Example: The carol singers put everyone on the street in the holiday spirit.
wind down
Definition: (phrasal verb) To relax and rest after a busy or exciting period, or to slowly come to an end.
Example: A warm bath helps me wind down after a long day.
