Vocabulary Study Guide

Click on each word to see its definition and example

caught my attention
Definition: (expression) Something that caught my attention seemed interesting or surprising enough to notice.
Example: The bright yellow poster caught my attention as soon as I walked into the store.
celebration of life
Definition: (expression) An event or occasion that honors and celebrates someone's life, rather than only mourning their death.
Example: The funeral felt more like a celebration of life than a sad goodbye.
nourishes
Definition: (verb) Provides the food or care something needs to stay healthy or alive.
Example: A balanced diet nourishes both the body and the mind.
blew my mind
Definition: (idiom) Amazed or shocked me, in a very impressive way.
Example: The size of the ancient temple absolutely blew my mind.
believe in
Definition: (expression) To have faith or confidence that something is true, real, or worthwhile.
Example: Many cultures believe in honoring their ancestors through yearly rituals.
showing respect
Definition: (expression) Behaving in a way that demonstrates honor or consideration for someone.
Example: Removing your shoes before entering is a way of showing respect in many homes.
labor of love
Definition: (idiom) A task done out of care or affection rather than for money or reward.
Example: Restoring the old cottage by hand was truly a labor of love for them.
took forever
Definition: (expression) Seemed to take a very long time.
Example: Waiting in that line at the airport took forever.
pitching in
Definition: (phrasal verb) Helping or contributing effort to a group task.
Example: The whole neighborhood was pitching in to clean up after the storm.
overlapping
Definition: (adjective) Partly covering or coinciding with something else.
Example: The two schedules kept overlapping, so she had to choose one event.
stuck out
Definition: (phrasal verb) Was especially noticeable compared to everything else.
Example: One detail in the report really stuck out to the manager.
fell apart
Definition: (phrasal verb) Broke into pieces; came apart suddenly.
Example: The old cardboard box fell apart as soon as she picked it up.
goes downhill
Definition: (idiom) Gets worse, often quickly.
Example: Once the rain starts, this trail usually goes downhill fast — literally and figuratively.
takeaway
Definition: (noun) The main point, lesson, or impression someone gets from something.
Example: The biggest takeaway from the meeting was that deadlines need to be clearer.
go for it
Definition: (idiom) Used to encourage someone to try something, without hesitation.
Example: If you've always wanted to try skydiving, just go for it.
