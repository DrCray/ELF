Getting Started

Talk about technology in the workplace; from coffee machines and copiers to computers and group chats.

Technology in the Office or Workplace

Technology has increased efficiency and flexibility in the workplace. Technology has made it easy to work remotely, which has increased the productivity of the employees. External and internal communication has become faster through emails and communication apps. Automation has saved time, and there is also a reduction of redundancy in tasks. Robots are now being used to manufacture products that consistently deliver the same product without defect. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning technology are innovations that are being deployed across industries.

Technology has wiped out the manual way of storing files. Now files are stored in the cloud, which can be accessed at any time and from anywhere. With technology, companies can make quick decisions, act faster towards solutions, and remain adaptable. Technology has optimized the use of resources and connected businesses worldwide. For example, customers based in America, can have services and products delivered from India. They can communicate with each other in an instant.

Most companies uses business technology like virtual meeting tools, corporate social networks, tablets, and smart customer relationship management applications, all of which accelerate the fast movement of data and information.

Technology in Education

Technology has greatly improved the education industry. With technology, students and parents have a variety of learning tools at their fingertips. Teachers can coordinate with classrooms across the world and share their ideas and resources online. Students can get immediate access to an abundance of information on the Internet. Teachers and students can access secondary and primary resources on the web and utilize them for their homework, projects, and research. Online learning has truly changed our entire perception of education.

The COVID-19 pandemic brought a paradigm shift using technology where school-going kids could continue their studies from home, while schools utilized virtual classrooms and teachers facilitated with real-time group video meetings, video lectures, chats, and file-sharing. All these have increased communication and collaboration significantly.

As well, students have acquired 21st-century skills and tools, with hands on experience with industrial machinery, software programming, AR (Augmented Reality), robots, and medical instruments. Technological skills have become a necessity for entry-level and high-level jobs alike.
