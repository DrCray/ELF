Vocabulary Study Guide

Click on each word to see its definition and example

gives me chills
Definition: (expression) Makes me feel suddenly scared, shocked, or deeply moved, as if I were cold.
Example: That old photo of the empty hospital always gives me chills.
hiding in plain sight
Definition: (idiom) Not noticed even though easy to see, often because it looks normal or harmless.
Example: The spare key was hiding in plain sight under the welcome mat.
took advantage of
Definition: (phrasal verb) Used someone's trust, kindness, or weakness unfairly to get what you wanted.
Example: The fake repairman took advantage of the old man's kindness and stole his wallet.
track record
Definition: (collocation) All the past successes or failures of a person or organization.
Example: The company has a strong track record of finishing projects on time.
red flags
Definition: (idiom) Warning signs that something may be wrong or dangerous.
Example: A job offer that asks for your bank password has serious red flags.
wrap my head around
Definition: (idiom, informal) Understand something that is strange, difficult, or shocking.
Example: I still can't wrap my head around how fast the year went by.
deep down
Definition: (expression) In your true, hidden feelings or nature, even if you don't show it.
Example: He acts tough, but deep down he's very shy.
put two and two together
Definition: (idiom) Understand the truth by connecting the facts you have noticed.
Example: When she saw the empty cookie jar and her son's messy face, she put two and two together.
piece together
Definition: (phrasal verb) Build an understanding of something slowly by collecting separate pieces of information.
Example: Detectives used phone records to piece together the night of the robbery.
get away with
Definition: (phrasal verb) Do something wrong without being caught or punished.
Example: He thought he could get away with copying his friend's homework.
in hindsight
Definition: (expression) When you think about a past event now, with the knowledge you have now.
Example: In hindsight, we should have left for the airport an hour earlier.
point out
Definition: (phrasal verb) Tell someone a fact or mention something so that they notice it.
Example: My teacher was kind enough to point out the mistakes in my essay.
make sense of
Definition: (expression) Understand something that is confusing or hard to explain.
Example: It took me an hour to make sense of the instructions for my new printer.
on the other hand
Definition: (expression) Used to introduce a different or opposite point of view.
Example: The city is exciting. On the other hand, it's very expensive to live there.
food for thought
Definition: (idiom) Something that makes you think seriously about an idea.
Example: The speaker's talk about online privacy gave everyone food for thought.
