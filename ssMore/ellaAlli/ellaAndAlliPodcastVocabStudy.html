Vocabulary Study Guide

Click on each word to see its definition and example

spinning
Definition: (idiom) Feeling confused or overwhelmed, as if the head is turning.
Example: After reading the plot twist, my head was spinning for the rest of the day.
wearing a wire
Definition: (idiom) Secretly recording a conversation using a hidden microphone, often to gather evidence.
Example: The undercover officer was wearing a wire during the entire meeting with the suspect.
run the show
Definition: (idiom) To be in charge and make all the important decisions.
Example: Ever since the manager retired, her assistant has basically been running the show.
crashing down
Definition: (idiom) To suddenly collapse or fail completely, often after a period of success.
Example: His entire business came crashing down after the scandal was made public.
green with envy
Definition: (idiom) Extremely jealous of someone else.
Example: She was green with envy when her coworker got the promotion she wanted.
out for a quick buck
Definition: (idiom) Trying to make easy money fast, often dishonestly.
Example: That contractor is just out for a quick buck; don't trust his estimate.
swept her off her feet
Definition: (idiom) To make someone fall in love very quickly and intensely.
Example: He swept her off her feet with flowers and a surprise trip to Paris.
head over heels
Definition: (idiom) Completely and deeply in love.
Example: They've only been dating a month, but he's already head over heels.
played like a fiddle
Definition: (idiom) To be easily tricked or manipulated by someone.
Example: The scammer had the whole town played like a fiddle before anyone noticed.
hog the spotlight
Definition: (idiom) To take all the attention for oneself.
Example: He always tries to hog the spotlight at family gatherings, even at other people's parties.
dig up dirt
Definition: (idiom) To search for embarrassing or incriminating information about someone.
Example: The journalist spent months trying to dig up dirt on the mayor's finances.
catches wind of
Definition: (idiom) To hear about or become aware of something, often something secret.
Example: As soon as the press caught wind of the merger, stock prices jumped.
go off the rails
Definition: (idiom) To suddenly start behaving in an uncontrolled or unexpected way.
Example: The meeting completely went off the rails once the two managers started arguing.
throw in the towel
Definition: (idiom) To give up on something.
Example: After three failed attempts, he finally threw in the towel and hired a professional.
get to the bottom of it
Definition: (idiom) To discover the real truth or cause of a problem.
Example: The detective promised the family she would get to the bottom of it.
keep her cool
Definition: (idiom) To remain calm, especially under pressure.
Example: Even during the loud argument, the negotiator managed to keep her cool.
spilled the beans
Definition: (idiom) To accidentally or carelessly reveal a secret.
Example: My little brother spilled the beans about the surprise party before we even left the house.
swooped in
Definition: (idiom) To arrive suddenly and take action, often to help or intervene.
Example: Security swooped in the moment they saw the shoplifter leave the store.
off the hook
Definition: (idiom) No longer blamed or responsible for something.
Example: Once the real culprit confessed, she was finally off the hook.
patch things up
Definition: (idiom) To resolve a disagreement and restore a good relationship.
Example: The two old friends finally sat down to patch things up after years of silence.
