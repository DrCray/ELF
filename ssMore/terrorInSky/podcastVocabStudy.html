Vocabulary Study Guide

Click on each word to see its definition and example

sent shivers down my spine
Definition: (idiom) Made me feel very frightened or nervous, almost like a cold feeling running down my back.
Example: The strange noise in the empty house sent shivers down my spine.
nervous flyer
Definition: (collocation) A person who feels worried or afraid when traveling by plane.
Example: My mother is a nervous flyer, so she always chooses a seat near the front.
out of nowhere
Definition: (expression) Suddenly and without any warning.
Example: A deer ran onto the road out of nowhere, and I had to brake hard.
count themselves lucky
Definition: (expression) Feel thankful because their situation could have been much worse.
Example: The hikers lost their way in the storm, but they can count themselves lucky that a farmer found them.
fall apart
Definition: (phrasal verb) To lose control of your emotions and become unable to deal with a situation.
Example: She was calm at the hospital, but she started to fall apart when she got home.
stuck to
Definition: (phrasal verb) Continued doing what was planned or expected, without changing.
Example: Even when it got difficult, the team stuck to the plan and finished the project on time.
muscle memory
Definition: (collocation) The ability to do something automatically, without thinking, because you have practiced it many times.
Example: After years of practice, playing the piano became muscle memory for him.
get to the bottom of
Definition: (idiom) To find out the true cause of a problem or situation.
Example: The manager promised to get to the bottom of the missing money.
turned out
Definition: (phrasal verb) Was discovered to be true in the end, often surprisingly.
Example: The noise in the kitchen turned out to be our cat knocking over a cup.
slipped through the cracks
Definition: (idiom) Was missed or not noticed because a system or process failed to catch it.
Example: Her application slipped through the cracks, and nobody called her back for weeks.
cut corners
Definition: (idiom) To do something in the cheapest or fastest way, often by ignoring rules or safety.
Example: The builders tried to cut corners, and now the roof leaks every time it rains.
point the finger at
Definition: (idiom) To blame someone for something bad that happened.
Example: It's easy to point the finger at the new employee, but the whole team made mistakes.
pushed aside
Definition: (phrasal verb) Treated as less important and ignored.
Example: His health was pushed aside while he worked long hours to finish his degree.
take responsibility
Definition: (collocation) To accept that something is your job or your fault and deal with it.
Example: Good leaders take responsibility when things go wrong instead of blaming others.
take for granted
Definition: (expression) To expect something to always be there, so you stop noticing or valuing it.
Example: We often take for granted clean water until the supply is cut off.
