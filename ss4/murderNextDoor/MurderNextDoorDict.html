Murder Next Door?


  1. Two neighbors had been jogging by someone’s house and noticed what appeared to be a dead body.
  2. It was leaning against the wall right next to the garage door, wrapped in black garbage bags, and tightly wound with gray duct tape.
  3. They called the police to report the incident.
  4. Flora Potts had dropped her kids off at school earlier and was arriving home when she noticed her house was surrounded by police.
  5. In a panic, She turned her car around and drove straight to her lawyer’s office.
  6. After calling the police, the lawyer told Flora that they had found a body in her backyard.
  7. Flora began to laugh and told him that it was a Halloween decoration for their fake crime scene.
  8. Dan, the lawyer, said “Well, it must look pretty real. It fooled your neighbors and the police.”
