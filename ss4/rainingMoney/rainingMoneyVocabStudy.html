Vocabulary Study Sheet

Read the definition, then reveal the examples to see the word in context.

Vehicle
Definition: A machine used to transport people or goods, such as a car, truck, or bus.

1. The family decided to rent a larger vehicle for their camping trip so everyone would have enough room to sit comfortably.

2. Before purchasing a new vehicle, it is important to check its safety ratings and fuel efficiency.

Flying around
Definition: Moving through the air in different directions quickly or chaotically.

1. During the autumn festival, children laughed as they watched colorful kites flying around in the windy sky.

2. The sudden gust of wind sent important papers flying around the office, causing a lot of confusion.

Sticks
Definition: Attaches firmly to a surface or object.

1. If you touch the wet paint on the bench, it will stick to your fingers and make a mess.

2. The strong magnet easily sticks to the refrigerator door, holding up the grocery list.

Windshield
Definition: The window at the front of a vehicle that protects the driver and passengers from the wind and weather.

1. The driver turned on the wipers to clear the heavy rain from the windshield so he could see the road.

2. A small rock hit the truck's windshield, leaving a tiny crack in the glass.

Scattering
Definition: Spreading or throwing things in different directions over a wide area.

1. The farmer was scattering seeds across the field so the crops would grow evenly in the spring.

2. When the loud firecracker went off, the flock of birds went scattering in all directions.

All over
Definition: Covering or spread across the entire surface or area of something; everywhere.

1. The children had so much fun playing in the mud that they got dirty all over their clothes and faces.

2. After the arts and crafts session, glitter was scattered all over the classroom floor.

Piling up
Definition: Accumulating or forming a heap of things on top of one another.

1. After the weekend festival, trash was piling up in the trash cans and needed to be collected.

2. She had so much homework to do that her books and papers started piling up on her desk.

React
Definition: To behave in a particular way in response to a situation or event.

1. The teacher was curious to see how the students would react to the surprise pop quiz.

2. When the dog barked loudly, the cat reacted by running to hide under the sofa.

Case
Definition: A specific instance of an event or situation.

1. In this case, we will need to call a professional plumber because the pipe is completely broken.

2. The detective solved the difficult case after months of following clues and interviewing witnesses.

Jackets
Definition: Outer garments worn over the body to keep warm or for protection.

1. Make sure you wear your jackets today because the weather forecast predicts a sudden drop in temperature.

2. They hung their heavy winter jackets in the closet as soon as they entered the warm cabin.

Armored
Definition: Covered with strong metal or other tough materials to protect against attacks or damage.

1. The medieval knight rode into battle on an armored horse to avoid being injured by enemy swords.

2. The president travels in an armored limousine to ensure maximum safety during public events.

Properly
Definition: In a correct, suitable, or appropriate manner.

1. The mechanic explained that the brakes were not installed properly, which caused the strange noise.

2. If you don't store perishable food properly in the refrigerator, it will spoil quickly.

Gap
Definition: A space or opening between two objects or surfaces.

1. There was a small gap between the fence and the wall where the tiny kitten managed to escape.

2. The dentist noticed a noticeable gap between the child's two front teeth.

Frame
Definition: The rigid supporting structure around which something is built or attached.

1. The beautiful oil painting was placed in a wooden frame before being hung in the gallery.

2. The mechanic checked the car's frame for any signs of damage after the minor collision.

Latch
Definition: A mechanical device used to keep a door, gate, or window securely closed.

1. The garden gate has a simple latch that can be easily opened by lifting the metal lever.

2. Please make sure the latch on the hamster cage is secure so the pet doesn't escape during the night.

Routes
Definition: Established or regular paths taken to get from one place to another.

1. The bus driver takes different routes depending on the time of day to avoid heavy traffic.

2. The postal worker memorized all the routes in the neighborhood to deliver the mail efficiently.

Repairs
Definition: The act of fixing something that is broken, damaged, or not working correctly.

1. The old bridge needs immediate repairs before it is safe for cars to drive over it again.

2. After the severe storm, the roof of the house required several repairs to stop the leaks.

Bulletproof
Definition: Designed to prevent bullets from passing through.

1. The bank teller was protected behind thick, bulletproof glass during the attempted robbery.

2. Soldiers in the field rely on bulletproof vests to protect them from gunfire.

Spilled
Definition: Caused a substance to fall out of a container and spread over a surface.

1. The waiter accidentally spilled coffee all over the restaurant floor when he tripped over a chair.

2. The bag of flour tore open and spilled its contents across the kitchen counter.

Turned in
Definition: To return, give back, or hand over something (often lost or stolen property) to the authorities or the rightful owner.

1. After finding a lost wallet on the park bench, the honest student turned it in to the police station so the owner could be found.

2. The teacher reminded the students that anyone who found the missing textbook should turn it in to the lost and found box.

Prank
Definition: A trick or joke played on someone to make them look foolish or to surprise them.

1. The students played a harmless prank on their teacher by turning all the desks upside down before class.

2. He thought the strange phone call was just a prank by his friends, but it turned out to be a serious emergency.

Integrity
Definition: The quality of being honest and having strong moral principles.

1. The judge was known for his integrity and fairness in every trial he oversaw.

2. A good leader must demonstrate integrity to earn the trust and respect of their team.

Finders-keepers
Definition: A rule or concept stating that whoever finds something first is entitled to keep it.

1. The children argued over the lost toy, with one of them shouting "finders-keepers" because he picked it up first.

2. Although she found a twenty-dollar bill on the sidewalk, she didn't believe in "finders-keepers" and asked the store clerk if anyone had lost it.

Committed a crime
Definition: To have done something illegal or against the law.

1. The judge sentenced the man to five years in prison because he had committed a crime.

2. When the teenager hacked into the school's computer system, he didn't realize he had just committed a crime.

Incident
Definition: A specific event or occurrence, often one that is unusual or noteworthy.

1. The school principal sent a letter home to parents explaining the incident that happened during recess.

2. No one was injured during the minor incident at the factory, but work was stopped for the day.

Car tags
Definition: The metal or plastic plates attached to a vehicle for official identification; license plates.

1. The police officer wrote down the car tags of the vehicle that ran the red light.

2. You must renew your car tags every year at the Department of Motor Vehicles.

Negligence
Definition: The failure to take proper care of something, resulting in damage or loss.

1. The company was sued for negligence after failing to maintain safe working conditions for its employees.

2. The doctor lost his medical license due to negligence that led to a patient getting severely ill.

Long gone
Definition: Having left a place a long time ago, or unable to be recovered or found.

1. By the time the police arrived at the bank, the robbers were already long gone.

2. I'm afraid the concert tickets are long gone; they sold out completely within the first five minutes.
